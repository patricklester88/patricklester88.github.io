About WhereAmI

WhereAmI helps you quickly discover your approximate location, understand how GPS works, and generate a simple location certificate directly in your browser.

Our mission

We created WhereAmI to make location information easy to understand. Whether you're confirming where you are, learning about GPS technology or helping someone find you, the website aims to provide clear, practical information without requiring an app.

Fast

Works directly in your browser using your device's location services.

Educational

Includes guides explaining GPS, Wi‑Fi positioning, browser permissions and location accuracy.

Privacy

Your browser asks permission before sharing your location. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Useful

Create a printable location certificate and share map links when appropriate.

Future development

  • Optional user accounts and saved certificates.
  • Improved certificate designs.
  • More GPS learning guides.
  • Support for pets, luggage and emergency contact profiles.
