What are browser location permissions?

Modern browsers ask for your permission before sharing your location with a website. This gives you control over when your location is shared.

WhereAmI only receives your location if you choose Allow.

Why do websites ask for my location?

📍 Current location

Show your position on a map.

🗺 Directions

Provide navigation from your current position.

📄 Certificates

Create a downloadable location certificate.

🌦 Local services

Show nearby information such as weather or places.

What happens when I click Allow?

  1. Your browser requests your device's location.
  2. Your device may use GPS, Wi-Fi, mobile networks or IP information.
  3. Your browser sends an estimated location to the website.
  4. The website uses that information to provide its service.

Can I refuse?

Yes. If you choose Block or Don't Allow, the website will not receive your location and some location-based features may not work.

Can I change my decision later?

BrowserTypical location settings
ChromeSite Settings → Location
EdgeSite Permissions → Location
FirefoxPrivacy & Security → Permissions
SafariWebsite Settings → Location

Privacy

WhereAmI displays the location information supplied by your browser. Review the site's privacy policy to understand how any information is handled.

Safety tips

  • Only allow trusted websites.
  • Review permissions regularly.
  • Use secure HTTPS websites.
  • Remove permissions when no longer needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will websites know my exact address?

Not always. Accuracy depends on your device and surroundings.

Can I allow access only once?

Many browsers let you allow access for one visit or remember your decision.

Why didn't I see a prompt?

You may already have granted or denied permission, or location services may be disabled.
