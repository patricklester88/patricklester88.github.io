🎯 Why Your Location Can Be Wrong

Understanding why GPS and browser locations are sometimes incorrect.

Why isn't my location always correct?

Your device estimates its position using GPS satellites, Wi‑Fi networks, mobile phone masts and internet information. Sometimes these sources disagree, resulting in an inaccurate location.

🏢 Tall buildings

Signals reflect from buildings, reducing GPS accuracy.

🏠 Indoors

Walls block satellites so devices often rely on Wi‑Fi.

🌳 Trees

Dense foliage weakens satellite signals.

📶 Wi‑Fi positioning

Incorrect Wi‑Fi databases can place you in the wrong location.

📡 Mobile networks

Phone mast positioning is usually less precise than GPS.

💻 VPN or IP location

Your internet connection may appear to come from another town or city.

📱 Device quality

Older devices generally produce less accurate results.

🛰 Too few satellites

Fewer visible satellites usually means poorer accuracy.

How can I improve accuracy?

  • Go outdoors.
  • Enable Wi‑Fi and location services.
  • Wait a few seconds.
  • Avoid tall buildings where possible.
  • Keep your device updated.
