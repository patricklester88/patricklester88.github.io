Cookies and browser controls

Cookie Policy

This policy explains what cookies are, how WhereAmI may use them, and how you can control them through your browser.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can place on your device. They may help a website remember settings, understand how visitors use the site, or support advertising and analytics services.

Similar technologies, such as local storage or device identifiers, may sometimes perform related functions.

How WhereAmI may use cookies

Essential cookies

These may be used where necessary for security, basic website operation or essential preferences.

Analytics cookies

If analytics are introduced, they may help us understand which pages are visited and how the website can be improved.

Advertising cookies

If services such as Google AdSense are used, cookies may help select, deliver and measure advertisements.

Preference technologies

These may remember consent choices or display settings.

Google advertising services

WhereAmI may use Google advertising services. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalise and measure advertisements under their own policies.

Depending on your location, you may be shown a consent message before non-essential advertising cookies are used.

Advertising should not prevent access to the main location tool or educational content.

Third-party services

Some functions may rely on third-party services such as hosting, maps, address lookup, analytics or advertising. These services may set their own cookies or process technical information according to their own policies.

Your choices

  • Block all cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Allow cookies only for selected websites.
  • Use private or incognito browsing.
  • Change advertising or consent preferences where offered.

Blocking some cookies may affect how certain website features operate, although the main informational pages should remain accessible.

Browser controls

Google Chrome

Open Settings, then Privacy and security, followed by Third-party cookies or Site settings.

Microsoft Edge

Open Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.

Mozilla Firefox

Open Settings, then Privacy & Security.

Safari

Open Settings or Preferences, then Privacy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website introduces new services, analytics, advertising tools or legal requirements.
