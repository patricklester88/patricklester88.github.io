Contact Us

WhereAmI is a free browser-based location tool designed to help users quickly view their current location and better understand GPS technology.

If you've found an issue, have an idea for a new feature, noticed incorrect information, or simply want to provide feedback, please get in touch.

📧 whereami3011@gmail.com

What can you contact us about?

  • General questions about the website
  • GPS or location-related enquiries
  • Bug reports
  • Feature suggestions
  • Reporting incorrect information
  • Accessibility improvements
  • General feedback

Reporting a problem

If you're reporting an issue, it helps if you include:

  • Your device (Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone etc.)
  • Your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari etc.)
  • What happened
  • Any error messages
  • A screenshot if available

Important

WhereAmI is provided as an informational tool and should not be relied upon for emergency, medical or safety-critical situations.

If you require emergency assistance, always contact your local emergency services directly.
