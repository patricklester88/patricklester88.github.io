Frequently asked questions

Is WhereAmI free?

Yes. The basic location tool and guides are free to use.

Does it work on phones and computers?

Yes, although smartphones often provide more precise results because many include dedicated GPS hardware.

Why is my location not exact?

Accuracy can be affected by buildings, indoor use, device hardware and the positioning sources available.

Can I save the certificate as a PDF?

Choose Print / Save as PDF and select your browser's PDF destination.

Does WhereAmI control my GPS?

No. It displays the location information supplied by your browser and device.
