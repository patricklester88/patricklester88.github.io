Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how WhereAmI handles location information, certificates, cookies and third-party services.

Our approach

WhereAmI is designed to be simple and privacy-conscious. The website only asks your browser for location access when you choose to use a location feature.

You remain in control of whether your browser shares your location.

Location information

Permission first

Your browser asks for permission before sharing location data with the website.

Device-generated data

Your device may use GPS, Wi-Fi, mobile networks or IP information to estimate your position.

No guaranteed precision

Location results are estimates and may be affected by your device, surroundings and signal quality.

Your browser controls access

You can block or later remove location permission in your browser settings.

Storage of location data

WhereAmI is designed to display the location supplied by your browser for the feature you request. The site does not intentionally create a permanent server-side history of your location.

However, technical service providers involved in hosting, mapping, analytics or advertising may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Do not use WhereAmI to handle information that you consider highly sensitive or confidential.

Location certificates

If you create or download a location certificate, it is generated for your own use. You are responsible for how you save, store, print or share it.

A certificate may contain coordinates, an approximate address, accuracy information, date and time. It should not be treated as guaranteed legal proof of location.

Cookies and similar technologies

WhereAmI may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality.

If analytics or advertising services are used, those services may set their own cookies or process technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate region.

For more information, please read the Cookie Policy.

Third-party services

The website may rely on third-party services for hosting, maps, address lookup, analytics or advertising. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

WhereAmI is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites reached through links from this site.

Children's privacy

WhereAmI is intended as a general information and utility website. Children should use location features with the knowledge and supervision of a parent, guardian or responsible adult.

Your choices

  • Allow or block browser location access.
  • Remove previously granted permissions.
  • Control cookies through your browser settings.
  • Choose whether to download or share a certificate.
  • Contact us with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
