WhereAmI?

How WhereAmI? handles location and privacy.

Privacy Policy

WhereAmI? is designed to be simple and privacy-conscious. The website asks your browser for location permission only when you choose to use the location feature.

Location data

Your browser controls whether location is shared. If you decline permission, the website cannot access your location.

This demo does not intentionally store your location on a server. Some third-party services, such as maps, hosting, analytics or advertising services, may process technical data according to their own policies.

Certificates

If you download a certificate, it is generated for your own use. You are responsible for how you store or share it.

Cookies and advertising

If advertising, analytics or affiliate links are added, cookies or similar technologies may be used. See the Cookie Policy for more information.
